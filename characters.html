The Karate Kid (2010) features a group of characters who help shape Dre Parker’s journey. The table below provides details about the main characters, the actors who portray them, and their importance to the story.

Character Name Actor Description Role in the Story
Dre Parker Jaden Smith A young boy who moves to China and struggles to fit in. Main character who learns kung fu and personal discipline.
Mr. Han Jackie Chan A quiet maintenance man with a painful past. Dre’s mentor who teaches kung fu, respect, and balance.
Meiying Wenwen Han A talented violinist and Dre’s friend. Provides emotional support and friendship.
Cheng Zhenwei Wang A skilled kung fu student and Dre’s main rival. Represents the main conflict Dre must overcome.
