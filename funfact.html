The Karate Kid (2010) includes many interesting behind-the-scenes details that helped make the film unique. Below are some fun facts about the movie, followed by the official trailer.

Fun Facts

  • The movie was filmed in several locations across China, including Beijing.
  • Despite the title, the martial art featured in the film is kung fu, not karate.
  • Jackie Chan performed many of his own stunts in the movie.
  • Jaden Smith trained for several months to prepare for the role.
  • The film is a reimagining of the original 1984 movie.
  • The movie emphasizes discipline, respect, and balance rather than violence.
